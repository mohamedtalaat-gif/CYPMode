## biology

### bio_p450_cycle: The catalytic cycle of a cytochrome P450

The eight steps by which a P450 oxidises a substrate RH to ROH with one oxygen atom from O2 and two electrons from NADPH. A molecule that competes for step 2, or that binds the iron so that step 4 cannot happen, switches the enzyme off while it is bound (reversible inhibition). A molecule that the enzyme itself converts in steps 6 to 8 into something that stays attached to the heme or the protein switches it off for good: that is time-dependent inhibition, the label this project predicts. In the middle, the real heme and the axial cysteine of CYP3A4 rendered from its crystal structure (PDB 6MA7); at the top, a real substrate of the enzyme, testosterone, and its classic CYP3A4 product, 6-hydroxytestosterone.

### ml_chemeleon_stage1: The stage-1 screen of the fine-tuned networks against its rule

The first stage of the staged design of the fine-tuned networks (CYP3A4, repeat 0, 5 outer folds, confidence cut z0 = 1.0). The rule was written before any result, for a reason of cost (about 38 minutes per task): the second stage runs only if the mean gain in AUC reaches 0.005 and the stack is better in at least 4 of the 5 folds. ### ml_design_floor: The smallest gain a gate can certify

The smallest gain in AUC that the corrected resampled t can certify (two-sided p < 0.05), as a function of the number of folds J, for the standard deviation of the fold differences that the logged comparisons imply (0.0055, the median of 156 comparisons). With 5 outer folds and one repeat (J = 5) it is 0.0103; with 5 outer folds and 3 repeats (J = 15, the design of the gates) 0.0067; with 10 outer folds and 3 repeats (J = 30, the replicate) 0.0043. The variance factor 1/J + n_test/n_train never falls below the test fraction, so more repeats shrink the floor only so far: a gain of a few thousandths of AUC cannot be certified by this design, whatever its truth. ### struct_openfold3_memory: OpenFold3 on a 16 GB machine: the memory arithmetic and the chunking that fixes it

Memory of one triangle-attention layer of the OpenFold3 pairformer against the number of tokens N (residues, ligand heavy atoms and the 43 atoms of the heme): 3 tensors x 4 heads x N rows x N x N x 4 bytes = 48 N^3 bytes. For bench-YNV, N = 592 and the formula gives 9.27 GiB, the size of the buffer the runtime refused in the first probe (log: Invalid buffer size 9.27 GiB). The runtime's working-set limit on this machine is 15.39 GiB and the weights and activations already occupy about half of it, so the layer cannot be allocated whole. Cutting the rows into chunks of 128 makes the layer need 2.01 GiB at the same N. Dots mark the 189 base inputs of this project (one per ligand), N from 560 to 603.

### struct_diffusion_denoising: Diffusion on a real ligand: from noise to a pose

Diffusion on a real ligand: the 24 heavy atoms of OCNT-2395712, a donor ligand of the official set, projected on the plane of their two principal axes. The denoiser knows two ways it can sit: Boltz-2's first sample with the iron-nitrogen restraint (pose A, blue) and without it (pose B, red), which differ by 5.7 A per atom on average after superposing the proteins. Forward, noise is mixed in: x_t = sqrt(a_t) x_0 + sqrt(1 - a_t) e, with a_t falling from 1 to 0 on a cosine schedule. Reverse, from pure noise, each of 40 steps estimates the clean pose as the posterior mean over the two poses given the noisy atoms and removes part of the noise. Different noise draws commit to different poses (top row: a start that ends in pose A; bottom row: one that ends in pose B), which is where the diversity of a pool of samples comes from. A real network replaces the hand-made denoiser with one learned from thousands of structures.

### kin_michaelis_menten: Michaelis-Menten saturation and its double-reciprocal form

Reaction rate v against substrate concentration [S]: v = Vmax [S] / (Km + [S]). The rate approaches Vmax; Km is the concentration that gives Vmax/2. The inset plots 1/v against 1/[S] (Lineweaver-Burk), where the same data fall on a straight line with slope Km/Vmax and intercept 1/Vmax.

### kin_inhibition_types: Reversible inhibition: competitive, non-competitive, uncompetitive and mixed

The same enzyme without inhibitor (solid) and with a fixed inhibitor concentration (dashed). With alpha = 1 + [I]/Ki and alpha' = 1 + [I]/Ki', the apparent constants are Vmax/alpha' and Km alpha/alpha'. Competitive (alpha > 1, alpha' = 1): Km rises, Vmax unchanged. Non-competitive (alpha = alpha'): Vmax falls, Km unchanged. Uncompetitive (alpha = 1, alpha' > 1): both fall by the same factor. Mixed (alpha > alpha' > 1): Km rises and Vmax falls.
