### arch_nested_cv: Nested cross-validation as a picture

Nested cross-validation as a grid. The compounds are cut into 5 outer folds; each outer fold in turn is held out and never used for any choice. The other 4 are cut again into 4 inner folds that give out-of-fold predictions on which the models are compared, the blend is fitted and the call threshold is set. The whole procedure is repeated with new random cuts (15 outer folds in all with the repeats), and the held-out scores are what is reported.

### mol_morgan_steps: The Morgan algorithm, radius by radius

The Morgan algorithm on one real training compound (22 heavy atoms; its identifier is withheld because the compound is in the blinded set). Radius 0 looks at each atom alone, radius 1 adds its bonded neighbours, radius 2 the neighbours of the neighbours; the highlighted atoms are the environment of one atom at each radius. ## biology

### bio_p450_cycle: The catalytic cycle of a cytochrome P450

The eight steps by which a P450 oxidises a substrate RH to ROH with one oxygen atom from O2 and two electrons from NADPH. A molecule that competes for step 2, or that binds the iron so that step 4 cannot happen, switches the enzyme off while it is bound (reversible inhibition). A molecule that the enzyme itself converts in steps 6 to 8 into something that stays attached to the heme or the protein switches it off for good: that is time-dependent inhibition, the label this project predicts. In the middle, the real heme and the axial cysteine of CYP3A4 rendered from its crystal structure (PDB 6MA7); at the top, a real substrate of the enzyme, testosterone, and its classic CYP3A4 product, 6-hydroxytestosterone.

### ml_design_floor: The smallest gain a gate can certify

The smallest gain in AUC that the corrected resampled t can certify (two-sided p < 0.05), as a function of the number of folds J, for the standard deviation of the fold differences that the logged comparisons imply (0.0055, the median of 156 comparisons). ### ml_decision_tree: A decision tree on real compounds

A decision tree of depth 3 (at least 40 compounds in a leaf) fitted to the 2334 labelled CYP3A4 compounds using only two descriptors, the octanol-water partition coefficient (MolLogP) and the polar surface area (TPSA). Left: the plane cut into the 8 rectangles the tree defines; a rectangle is shaded from blue (few time-dependent inhibitors in its leaf) to red (many) by the share of positives in its leaf, which is the probability the tree gives to every compound that falls in it; no leaf has a majority of positives, so a hard yes/no tree would answer no everywhere. ### struct_diffusion_denoising: Diffusion on a real ligand: from noise to a pose

Diffusion on a real ligand: the 24 heavy atoms of OCNT-2395712, a donor ligand of the official set, projected on the plane of their two principal axes. Forward, noise is mixed in: x_t = sqrt(a_t) x_0 + sqrt(1 - a_t) e, with a_t falling from 1 to 0 on a cosine schedule. A real network replaces the hand-made denoiser with one learned from thousands of structures.

### phys_koopmans: Orbital energies and ionisation potentials: Koopmans' relation on real compounds

Koopmans' approximation on the 6895 compounds of the physics cache: the ionisation potential computed from the energy of the cation (vertical, vertical axis) against minus the energy of the highest occupied orbital (horizontal).
