CYPMode

Time-dependent CYP inhibition and CYP3A4 structure prediction for the OpenADMET CYP blind challenge, with the evidence behind every number.

Read

  • Notebook

    The whole project from first principles, with a contents, a search, and the theme of the site.

  • Method report

    What is submitted, what was tried, and every result, in sections that open and close.

  • Validation reports

    The reports on how well the models hold up, with charts and tables that can be explored.

Explore

  • Explorer

    The ledger of gates, the selection rules, the poses of the benchmark, and the out-of-fold predictions, in the browser.

  • Map

    The modules, the queue, the flow of the data, and the kinds of idea that were tried.

  • Card

    The interactive card of the project.

  • Film

    The film of the project, 6.5 minutes.
