## Innovation summary

This section lists what the work adds, one claim at a time, with the evidence that stands next to it. Each row is a claim that the logs can check; the cross-validated numbers are estimates on the training data and not scores on the hidden labels (limitations L1, L2 and L13). ### Architecture: the label is modelled through its process

- **The label is a function of two measurements, so the models learn the measurements.** The TDI label is positive when `s = t - max(d, 4) > log10 2`. The rule reproduces every training label (0 mismatches for CYP3A4, 0 for CYP2D6). The stack therefore fits the shift `s` and the arms, and the label and its confidence `z = |s - log10 2| / sd(s)` follow from them, instead of fitting a binary label that discards the size of the margin.
- **A selection-aware CYP2D6 model.** CYP2D6 was measured only for compounds that an earlier screen promoted, so a model of the label alone answers a question about a selected population. The second file multiplies P(label | hit) by a gate P(hit). When the scored set looks like the training panel it raises the outer AUC from 0.5618 to 0.7278 (+0.1660, corrected p = 0.026); the gain disappears when the scored set holds no never-promoted compounds, and at the call rate of the final build the loss there is larger than the gain where it wins (section 7). The choice between the two files was made by a rule fixed before any upload, and the rule gives **file A** (the hits-only file); the other file is kept with its SHA-256.
- **Groups join a stack only through a gate.** 187 pre-registered verdicts in 59 families; 9 are MET and 178 are NOT MET. The final stacks are 28 (CYP3A4) and 23 (CYP2D6) candidates, selected by the logs and by nothing else.

### Data usage

- **A contaminated slice of the labels was found and removed from the CYP3A4 pool.** 1249 CYP3A4 rows have no direct-inhibition measurement and follow a different label convention than the blinded set; the pool keeps the 2334 rows with both arms, and every CYP3A4 number is computed on that population (section 2).
- **The evaluation reproduces the scored population.** The outer folds are weighted by similarity to the blinded structures and by the estimated prevalence, and only confidently labelled compounds are evaluated; 3 repeats of 5 outer folds (15 outer evaluations) with every selection step inside the inner folds (section 5).
- **One aggregate number is recovered from the public leaderboards.** The accuracy, precision and recall that the boards publish for every entry fix the positive rate of the scored set: 0.310 for CYP3A4 and 0.059 for CYP2D6. It sets the call rate (a multiple of the prevalence: 1.3 and 1.0); no label of any compound and no probing submission is used (limitation L13).
- **A check of that rate that reads no board.** Black-box label-shift estimation (a classifier that scores held-out compounds and the blinded structures on one scale, folds that keep chemical series whole, the held-out compounds weighted by structure similarity only) estimates the positive rate of the scored set from the training data. At z0 = 1.0 it gives 0.135 (0.043 to 0.286) for CYP2D6, against 0.059 in the final build; 0.352 (0.274 to 0.451) for CYP3A4, against 0.310 in the final build. The rule fixed before the first run (`cypmode/challenge/label_shift.py`) gives NOT INFORMATIVE for CYP2D6 (recovery error 0.68 of the rate is above 0.25); NOT INFORMATIVE for CYP3A4 (interval width 0.176 is above 0.5 times 0.310). The training data alone do not fix the rate to better than the width of these intervals, so the boards remain the source of the rate; the CYP2D6 interval contains the rate of the final build. the CYP3A4 interval contains the rate of the final build.
- **The official structure set was checked for stereochemistry.** 4 of the 20 ligands carry specified stereochemistry; only 23 of the 36 pool poses of those ligands reproduce it, so the pick is restricted to poses that do, and all 20 submitted poses reproduce the official stereochemistry (`stereo_check.log`).

### Uncertainty

- **Reliability by similarity to the training data, with intervals over chemical series.** Held-out compounds of the outer folds are cut by the nearest-training similarity; the interval of each bin resamples whole Butina clusters, so a bin is called supported only when the lower end of its interval reaches 0.60 (`applicability_domain.py`, `applicability_domain.log`). For CYP3A4 the 750 blinded compounds fall into 750 supported, 0 weak and 0 unsupported compounds; for CYP2D6 into 0 supported, 599 weak and 151 unsupported compounds, so the CYP2D6 predictions carry the weaker reliability.
- **Probabilities calibrated on the inner folds, with the failure reported.** The expected calibration error after isotonic regression is 0.0189 (CYP3A4) and 0.0054 (CYP2D6); the raw CYP2D6 blend has 0.3592 and is a ranking, not a probability (limitation L10).
- **A committee of models as an uncertainty signal was tested and did not help.** Added to the stack it moves the AUC by +0.0023 (p = 0.405) for CYP3A4 and -0.0070 (p = 0.615) for CYP2D6 at z0 = 1.0; its probability is miscalibrated as it comes out (expected calibration error 0.1410 and 0.3059). It is reported with the predictions and never alters one.

### Simple ideas that carry the structure track

- **A restraint of the iron-nitrogen bond for Type II ligands.** One extra constraint in the Boltz-2 input; the gates `restraint_bond` and `restraint_contact` are MET and MET on the crystal-anchored benchmark, and the same gates under the OpenStructure scorer give MET and MET.
- **The scorer's own validity call with a margin.** Every pose is checked by the 22 PoseBusters checks of the scorer, and a pose is eligible only when its smallest relative distance to the receptor is at least 0.8; a pose that fails a check scores zero, so validity is treated as a constraint and not as a feature.
- **A relaxation under a rule written before it was run.** The force-field relaxation of the ligand in the fixed pocket replaces a pose only when both pass the checks, the margin rises, the ligand moves at most 1.0 A and the iron-nitrogen distance changes at most 0.2 A; it was adopted for 20 of 20 ligands and raised the smallest margin from 0.801 to 0.849.

### Rigor

- **The pharmacophore of a pose read over a short trajectory, tested under a rule fixed before any number.** The ligand moves for 20 ps in the frozen pocket (protein and heme at their predicted positions, no water) for 104 poses of 10 benchmark ligands; it is not a dynophore of the literature, which follows the whole complex in water (`docs/structure_track/structure_ligand_dynamics.md`, limitation L16). Judged by the gate of the pose rules, none of the three scores met it (mean rho -0.214, +0.042, -0.071 against the bar of 0.3; the static model of the same poses gives -0.240), so the structure entry was not changed.
- **Negative results are results.** `NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md` lists all 178 verdicts that were NOT MET. The negative control, the whole nested procedure on permuted labels, gives an outer AUC of 0.5096 (CYP3A4) and 0.5012 (CYP2D6) at z0 = 1.0.
- **Every number in the reports is copied by code from a log and checked by the audit,** which is generated twice and compared byte for byte (section 4.4).

### What is not claimed

- The cross-validated AUC and MCC are estimates on the training data. The portal scores hidden labels, its test set sits closer to the training data than a random fold does, and the live board scores a part of it.
- The structure recipe does not claim a better pose choice: every rule for choosing among predicted poses is NOT MET. What it adds is the restraint, the validity margin, the stereochemistry check and the relaxation.
- The positive rates come from the public boards. The attempt to estimate them without a board is reported above with the verdict of its rule; limitation L13 states what depends on them.

## 2. Data and the label rule

The TDI label is a threshold of two measured pIC50 values, positive when `s = t - max(d, 4) > log10 2` (t: TDI-condition arm,
d: direct-inhibition arm, floor 4). The rule reproduces every training label exactly (audit section 4), including the
convention for compounds without a direct measurement; those rows follow a different convention than the scored test set and
are excluded from the CYP3A4 pool (audit section 4 gives the counts). The organisers confirmed the convention on the challenge Discord on 2026-10-29: a compound with a TDI arm and no direct arm
cannot show a shift, so it is labelled negative, and every test compound has both arms.

## 3. Architecture of the two tracks

This section says what the submitted models are, step by step. Every number is read from the code or from the final manifests when the report is generated. The notebook teaches each step from first principles; the section
that does is named at the end of the step.

### 3.1 TDI track (CYP3A4 and CYP2D6)

1. **Label.** A compound is positive when `s = t - max(d, 4) > log10 2`; the rule reproduces every training label (section 2; notebook 2.3). The confidence of a label is `z = |s - log10 2| / sd(s)` from the posterior standard
   deviations of the two arms, and only confidently labelled compounds are scored (notebook 2.5).
2. **Features, label-free.** The feature store holds 12,468 columns in 28 blocks (morgan_bits, morgan_c2, morgan_c3, fmorgan_c2, atompair_c, torsion_c, maccs, avalon, erg, rdkit_desc, motif, amine, ext_activity, tdi_alerts, tdi_alert_context, chemeleon, physics, physics_b, basic_centre, monroe, ext_cont, ext_tox, ext_adme, atomjepa, pp3a4, pp2d6, st3a4, st2d6). No block reads a label of a test compound; the leakage audit checks
   it (audit section 17; notebook Part III and Part X).
3. **The default stack: 23 candidates** of 7 families (1 extratrees, 3 knn_tanimoto, 11 lgbm_clf, 2 lgbm_reg_arms, 2 lgbm_reg_shift, 1 rf, 3 svm_tanimoto), each a model of the shift or of the label on one set of blocks (notebook 8.1).
4. **Validation.** Nested cross-validation: 3 repeats of 5 outer folds with 4 inner folds, seed 0, the plan saved with an identifier computed from the raw files. Every selection step (the blend, its penalty, the threshold) happens inside
   the inner folds, and the outer folds are weighted to resemble the scored population: similarity to the blinded structures, the estimated prevalence, only confident labels at two cut-offs (notebook 6.3 and 6.4).
5. **The blend.** A logistic regression on the ranks of the members, with the penalty (L1 or L2) and its strength (C in {0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0}) chosen on the inner folds; a deterministic solver (notebook 8.2 and 8.3).
6. **Which groups join.** A group of added candidates joins a stack only through a pre-registered gate (corrected resampled t, p < 0.05 at both confidence cuts, alone and inside the stack); a group found after looking also needs a
   replicate on ten outer folds (notebook 6.7 and 9.9). The final build reads the verdict lines of the logs and nothing else decides.
   - CYP3A4: final stack `default+all_survivors` of 28 candidates; groups that joined: chemprop_foundation, neural.
   - CYP2D6: final stack `default` of 23 candidates; groups that joined: none.
   - CYP3A4, `chemprop_foundation`: two Chemprop networks that start from the weights of pretrained OpenADMET models (one trained on MiniMol targets, one the Novartis ADMET surrogate) and are fine-tuned to the arm shift; run for CYP3A4 only, in two stages under a rule written before any result. Members: `chemeleon_minimol_mt_shift`, `chemeleon_surrogate_mt_shift`. Added to the default stack it raises the AUC by 0.0133 (corrected p = 0.0024) at the cut z0 = 1.0 and by 0.0144 (p = 0.0053) at z0 = 1.96.
   - CYP3A4, `neural`: the multitask network (one network over the assay arms, fitted to the label and to the arm shift) and plain Chemprop (a message-passing network on the molecular graph, fitted to the shift); two near-misses that joined into one lead on CYP3A4 and were confirmed on a replicate with ten outer folds. Members: `mlp_multitask_label`, `mlp_multitask_shift`, `chemprop_mt_shift`. Added to the default stack it raises the AUC by 0.0106 (corrected p = 0.0142) at the cut z0 = 1.0 and by 0.0106 (p = 0.0294) at z0 = 1.96.
   - CYP2D6: no group passed its gate, so the final stack is the default stack of its candidates.
   - These are passes of the registered rule. After the Holm adjustment over all stacked comparisons the smallest adjusted p-value is 0.26 (section 4.2), so they are reported as passes of the rule and not as effects that survive the number of attempts.
7. **Calibration and calls.** Probabilities by isotonic regression on the inner out-of-fold scores (notebook 8.5). The number of positive calls is a multiple of the estimated prevalence of the scored population, the multiple in
   {1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3} chosen by the highest mean outer-fold MCC (notebook 8.4): CYP3A4 1.3; CYP2D6 1.0.
8. **Two files.** A (hits-only CYP2D6 model) and B (the selection-aware CYP2D6 model, which multiplies P(label | hit) by a gate P(hit)); CYP3A4 is identical in both. The entry that is final is chosen by a rule declared in advance (`cypmode/challenge/selection_decision.py`, notebook 13.4); the rule gives file A, and file B is kept, with its SHA-256 in the manifest, to be scored on the test labels if the organisers publish them.

Figures: `docs/figures/architecture/arch_tdi_pipeline-light.png` and `arch_nested_cv-light.png` (dark versions beside them).

### 3.2 Structure track (CYP3A4, 20 ligands)

1. **Input.** The CYP3A4 sequence with its alignment, the heme with its covalent bond to the proximal cysteine, and the ligand, written as a Boltz-2 input (notebook 11.3).
2. **Donor restraint.** For a ligand with a Type II donor nitrogen the input carries a restraint of the iron-nitrogen bond; the gate that decided it is MET on the crystal-anchored benchmark (notebook 11.6).
3. **The pool.** Boltz-2 samples per ligand (the unguided and the restrained runs and 2 extra samples), 20 ligands, 200 poses in all; no second engine (engine consensus is NOT MET).
4. **Validity and margin.** Every pose is checked by the scorer's own PoseBusters call (22 checks); among the valid poses only those whose smallest relative distance to the receptor is at least 0.8 are eligible (notebook 11.4 and 11.10).
5. **The pick.** `first_valid`: the model's own ranking among the eligible poses; for a ligand with a Type II donor the restrained run is read first. Every rule for choosing a pose that was tested is NOT MET.
6. **Relaxation under a registered rule.** A force-field relaxation of the ligand in the fixed pocket replaces a pose only if both pass the 22 checks, the margin rises and is at least the floor, the ligand moves at most 1.0 A and the iron-nitrogen distance changes at most 0.2 A; adopted for 20 of 20 (notebook 11.10).
7. **The archive.** A deterministic zip of 20 complexes (the time of every entry fixed), SHA-256 `966c034c25373772...`.

Figures: `docs/figures/architecture/arch_structure_pipeline-light.png` (dark version beside it).

### 4.2 The accounting of the number of attempts

A gate controls the risk of admitting one idea that does not help; it does not control the risk that one of many ideas passes by chance. The 52 stacked comparisons of the batch are therefore also reported with the Holm
step-down adjustment (`cypmode/challenge/multiplicity.py`): 3 have a raw p-value below 0.05 (aux_chemprop_foundation_CYP3A4.log, aux_neural_CYP3A4.log, aux_neural_f10_CYP3A4.log), and the smallest adjusted p-value of the family is 0.26. A pass of a
pre-registered gate is therefore reported as a pass of the registered rule and not as an effect that survives the correction (notebook 9.11).

### 4.3 The controls

- **Negative control.** The labels of the pool are permuted and the whole nested procedure is repeated; the outer AUC must be 0.5 (notebook 9.10). Log: `permutation_control_*.log`.
- **Replicates.** A group found after looking at the 15-fold results (neural, pocket_cyp3a4, pocket_cyp2d6, site_cyp3a4, site_cyp2d6, hypothesis) joins only if the same comparison on ten outer folds is MET as well; the replicate was declared before any ten-fold number existed (notebook 9.9).
- **Design floor.** The least difference in AUC that the main design recognises 8 times in 10 is 0.0099, so a near miss below it is reported as inconclusive and not as a negative (notebook 6.7).
- **One ligand.** The two selection gates of the structure track recomputed with each ligand left out in turn, as a description (`NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md`).
- **Second looks.** A near miss may be looked at again, but only in a log that no gate parser reads (`exploratory_*`), so that it cannot change a model; the all-in-one stack of every cached candidate against the final stack is one of them.

## 5. Scoring-set-aware yardstick

Only confidently labeled compounds are scored. The confidence z = |s - log10 2| / sd(s) from the arms' posterior standard
deviations is used to restrict evaluation at two cut-offs (1.0 and 1.96), and evaluation weights follow similarity to the
unlabeled test structures and the estimated test prevalence. That prevalence is the one piece of information about the hidden
labels that the project uses: it is recovered from the accuracy, precision and recall that the public leaderboards publish for every
entry (see limitation L13 and the figure `data_prevalence_recovery`); no label of any compound and no probing submission is used.
Selection among models happens inside nested cross-validation and
models are compared by the corrected resampled t of Nadeau and Bengio (never by bootstrapping fixed predictions).

## 7. Selection-aware CYP2D6 model

CYP2D6 compounds were measured on this enzyme only if an earlier screen promoted them, so the training labels come from a selected subset and the scored set may hold compounds that would never have been promoted. The selection-aware model multiplies P(label | hit) by a gate P(hit). Against the hits-only stack it raises the outer AUC from 0.5618 to 0.7278 (+0.1660, corrected p = 0.026) when the scored set looks like the training panel. The gain depends on how many never-promoted compounds the scored set holds: it disappears when there are none, and the loss at the call rate of the final build is larger than the gain where it wins. The choice between the two files was therefore made by a procedure written before any upload; it gives **file A** (the hits-only file), and the other file is kept with its SHA-256 to be scored on the test labels if the organisers publish them.

## 11. Structure track

The structure levers gave 39 pre-registered verdicts, 5 of them MET (bond restraint, contact restraint, force-field relaxation of poses, pool gates repeated with OpenStructure's yardstick). Every rule for choosing a pose among the predicted ones was NOT MET on the crystal-anchored benchmark, so the pick is the model's own ranking among the valid poses; what the recipe adds is a restraint of the iron-nitrogen contact, the scorer's own validity call with a margin rule, and a registered relaxation.

**The crystal ligands of the gates.** The 20 official ligands have no public answer, so every gate of the structure track was measured on ligands whose crystal pose is public. The benchmark is 10 ligands picked from the 101 distinct CYP3A4 ligands of the PDB (`cypmode/validation/reference_ligands.py`) by a rule fixed in advance (`cypmode/validation/structure_benchmark.py`): 20 to 60 heavy atoms, resolution at most 3.0 A, organic atoms only and one fragment (74 of the 101 pass), then the most mutually dissimilar by a maximin pick on Morgan fingerprints that starts from the panel compounds already predicted (azamulin, clotrimazole, diltiazem, itraconazole, ketoconazole, ritonavir, troleandomycin, vardenafil, verapamil). The pick is deterministic and the list is not written by hand; with those seeds the function returns exactly these ligands (`tests/test_structure_benchmark.py`). They are, with the PDB entry of the copy used: MWS (6OOB, 27 heavy atoms, 2.20 A), TCI (9PLJ, 23 heavy atoms, 2.72 A), 2QH (4NY4, 34 heavy atoms, 2.95 A), A1CIW (9PLK, 32 heavy atoms, 2.25 A), YNV (7LXL, 46 heavy atoms, 2.75 A), PK9 (4D6Z, 23 heavy atoms, 1.93 A), TPF (6MA7, 22 heavy atoms, 2.09 A), MWV (6OO9, 36 heavy atoms, 2.25 A), A1ASV (9BV5, 22 heavy atoms, 2.71 A), A1A4S (9COV, 42 heavy atoms, 2.60 A). The models may have seen these entries in training, so the absolute accuracy on the benchmark is optimistic and it serves to compare settings and selection rules, not to forecast a score. The gate of the relaxation used 30 ligands: these 10 and the 20 ligands of the restraint-expansion plan registered on 2026-10-12 (every crystal ligand with a Type II donor, organic, one fragment, 8 to 30 heavy atoms, resolution at most 3.5 A and not in the benchmark; every eligible ligand was run and none was picked).

## 13. Limitations

### Audit extract: Limitations log

- **L1** (evaluation): All performance figures are cross-validated estimates on the training data. No figure here uses the hidden test labels. The stack AUC changes with the scored population: CYP3A4 0.7836 on all rows vs 0.8443 on confident rows, CYP2D6 0.6749 vs 0.7536. The leaderboard score of an entry is a separate measurement on the hidden labels, and the step from these estimates to it depends on the model (L2).
- **L2** (evaluation): The official baseline scores 0.3816 (CYP3A4) and 0.1761 (CYP2D6) on the live board, well above its cross-validated score on the same data, so cross-validation under-states leaderboard performance by an amount that depends on the model; the live board scores only part of the test set.
- **L3** (labels): The label rule was recovered from the data (mismatches: CYP3A4 0, CYP2D6 0). The rule that decides which compounds count as confidently labeled in the official score is not published; the confidence cut-off used here is a proxy and every analysis is repeated at two cut-offs.
- **L4** (uncertainty): Committee disagreement adds information beyond the decision margin for 1 components on CYP3A4 and 2 on CYP2D6 under the permutation test; where the count is zero the committee is reported as a measured negative result and does not alter predictions.
- **L5** (calibration): The probabilities the final build publishes (isotonic-calibrated) have calibration slopes of 0.9252 (CYP3A4) and 0.8249 (CYP2D6) on held-out folds. The sigmoid of the regularised blend as it comes out is a ranking score and not a probability (slopes 1.1813 and -0.0164); it is never published as one. Probabilities are calibrated to the weighted validation population, not to the unknown scored test population.
- **L6** (structure): On the diverse crystal benchmark the model's first-pick pose is 4.1929 A from the crystal pose on average; the structure score depends on a handful of compounds and is noisy. The benchmark ligands come from the PDB, which co-folding models may have seen, so absolute accuracy is optimistic.
- **L7** (reproducibility): The test suite has 1038 passing and 0 failing tests in this run. Multi-threaded LightGBM is not guaranteed bit-identical across thread counts (see the determinism section); the pipeline fixes seeds and thread counts, and cached predictions are checked against a fresh fit.
- **L8** (repository): The working tree has 0 modified and 0 untracked entries: results in this report come from uncommitted code until the work is committed.
- **L9** (population): The scored CYP2D6 set may contain many compounds the screen would never have promoted (scored as inactive). The selection-aware file gains AUC when they are numerous and loses both AUC and MCC when there are none, by more than it gains at the call rate of the final build (the table of job 016 in section 7), and neither the label-shift estimate nor the public metrics of the baseline identify their share (538 grid points are consistent with the metrics). The live board scores half of the test set and cannot separate the two files (job 016), so the entry is the hits-only file and the selection-aware file is kept, with its SHA-256, to be scored on the test labels if the organisers publish them: that comparison measures the share and tests the hypothesis on data that no choice of this project touched.
- **L10** (calibration): The CYP2D6 blend as it comes out is close to a constant (weighted expected calibration error 0.3592 against 0.0431 for CYP3A4): it ranks and transfers a call rate but is not a probability. The probabilities that are published pass through an isotonic step fitted on inner out-of-fold scores, which brings the error to 0.0054 (CYP2D6) and 0.0189 (CYP3A4); a monotone step restores calibration and adds no discrimination.
- **L11** (data): The CYP3A4 reaction-phenotyping and inhibition release of the laboratory that measured the challenge (Octant, same compound library) contains one compound that is also in the blinded test set (its identifier is withheld here, because the release rule forbids naming a blinded compound in a file that would be pushed). Its measurements are excluded from every use of that release and are never read by a model or a validation; the other rows of the release are used only for an independent check of the motif screen and never enter a prediction.
- **L12** (candidates): Two families of Chemprop candidates were run as a staged design under a rule written beforehand, for CYP3A4 only: CheMeleon was screened on 5 folds and not continued, because its gain (+0.0035 AUC) was below the bar of the stage rule, and the two OpenADMET pretraining weights (MiniMol targets and the Novartis ADMET surrogate) passed the screen and were run to the 15-fold gate, which they met. A candidate that was screened on 5 folds and not continued, or not run (CYP2D6), has no verdict: the report does not call it NOT MET, and the smallest gain that a 5-fold screen can certify is about one and a half times the smallest gain of the 15-fold design (see the design-floor figure). CYP2D6 was left out of the whole Chemprop design by a decision of 2026-10-16, taken before any CheMeleon measurement and confirmed on 2026-10-22: plain Chemprop is at chance on CYP2D6 (outer AUC 0.54 and 0.53) while the frozen CheMeleon block reaches 0.63, and the full design costs about 12 days of machine time (about 37 minutes per task, 80 tasks for each variant and enzyme) for a gain that was expected to be small: the first estimate of what the fine-tuned network could add, +0.004 to +0.008 AUC, was below the smallest difference of about 0.01 that the design can certify. The decision was taken before the result of the OpenADMET weights on CYP3A4 was known, so that whether they help on CYP2D6 is untested and not answered. The code and the weight files are in the repository.
- **L13** (data): The positive rate of the scored test set was recovered from the public leaderboards, which publish accuracy, precision and recall for every prediction file; the three numbers fix the rate (the implied rate agrees across all entries). It is aggregate information about the hidden labels that was available because the boards publish it: it weights the evaluation yardstick (the test prevalence) and sets the call rate (a multiple of the prevalence). No label of any compound and no per-compound information from a board was used, and no submission was made to probe a label. A model without this step would call the positive rate of the training data (about one fifth for both enzymes) instead of about one third (CYP3A4) and one in seventeen (CYP2D6), which costs MCC mostly on CYP2D6. A check that reads no board (a label-shift estimate from the training data and the blinded structures, `cypmode/challenge/label_shift.py`, with a rule fixed before its first run) is reported with its verdict in the innovation summary of the method report; the rate used by the final build rests on the boards.
- **L14** (candidates): The pocket block and the site model were tested with a conformer ensemble of 12 conformers within 5 kcal/mol of the lowest, which holds a member within 1.0 A of the crystal pose for only 12% of 131 crystal ligand copies; 300 conformers do so for 15%. Their negative verdicts are those of models that mostly did not see the bound conformation of the ligand. A wider ensemble was not tried.
- **L15** (structure): On the live board, which scores 10 of the 20 ligands, the submitted structure recipe scored an LDDT-PLI of 0.3623, below the organisers' Boltz-2 baseline (0.3950), with a lower BiSyRMSD (4.3220 A against 5.1870 A). The second candidate, the first eligible unguided pose, scored 0.3975 and did not pass the threshold registered before its upload. The gates of the structure track were measured on 30 crystal ligands that are chemically far from the 20 official ligands, so the evidence for the iron-nitrogen restraint may not transfer, and no rule for choosing a pose among the predicted ones passed its gate. The recipe is reported as submitted and makes no claim of a better LDDT-PLI than the baseline.
- **L16** (structure): The ligand dynamics in the frozen pocket ran 20 ps for each of 104 predicted poses of 10 benchmark ligands, with the protein and the heme held at their predicted positions and without water. It is not a dynophore of the literature, which follows the whole complex in water over nanoseconds, and it cannot show that a pose is wrong because the pocket would open, that a bridging water molecule is missing, or that a slow motion of the ligand changes the pose. The stability of a pose measures its agreement with the force field that also relaxed it. 0 of its three scores met the gate registered before the result (the highest mean rho was +0.042 against the bar of +0.30); 10 ligands cannot detect a modest effect, so the result is that this cheap dynamics did not rank the predicted poses of these ligands and says nothing about a dynamics of the whole complex or about the 20 official ligands, which are chemically far from the benchmark ligands.
